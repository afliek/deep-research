Q1 [3 marks]

EN:

(a) Express 1260 as a product of its prime factors, giving your answer in index notation.

(b) Hence find the highest common factor (HCF) and the lowest common multiple (LCM) of 1260 and 1512. Give your answers in index notation.

中文：

(a) 将 1260 表示为质因数的乘积，答案用指数形式表示。

(b) 由此求 1260 与 1512 的最大公因数（HCF）和最小公倍数（LCM），答案用指数形式表示。

Answer / 答案：

(a) 1260 = 2² × 3² × 5 × 7

(b) HCF = 2² × 3² × 7 = 252; LCM = 2³ × 3³ × 5 × 7 = 7560

解析：

步骤1（短除法分解质因数）：

1260 ÷ 2 = 630 → 630 ÷ 2 = 315 → 315 ÷ 3 = 105 → 105 ÷ 3 = 35 → 35 ÷ 5 = 7 → 7 ÷ 7 = 1

所以 1260 = 2² × 3² × 5 × 7。

步骤2：同理分解 1512：

1512 ÷ 2 = 756 → 756 ÷ 2 = 378 → 378 ÷ 2 = 189 → 189 ÷ 3 = 63 → 63 ÷ 3 = 21 → 21 ÷ 3 = 7 → 7 ÷ 7 = 1

所以 1512 = 2³ × 3³ × 7。

步骤3（HCF：取公共质因数的最低次幂）：

HCF = 2^min(2,3) × 3^min(2,3) × 7^min(1,1) = 2² × 3² × 7 = 4 × 9 × 7 = 252。

步骤4（LCM：取所有质因数的最高次幂）：

LCM = 2^max(2,3) × 3^max(2,3) × 5^max(1,0) × 7^max(1,1) = 2³ × 3³ × 5 × 7 = 8 × 27 × 35 = 7560。

步骤5（检验）：HCF × LCM = 252 × 7560 = 1 905 120；1260 × 1512 = 1 905 120，两者相等，结果正确。

Q2 [3 marks]

EN:

A sum of money is shared among three friends, Amira, Bala and Chen, in the ratio 5 : 3 : 2.

(a) Find the fraction of the total sum that Bala receives, giving your answer in its simplest form.

(b) Chen receives $240. Calculate the total sum of money shared and the amount that Amira receives.

中文：

一笔钱按 5 : 3 : 2 的比例分给三位朋友 Amira、Bala 和 Chen。

(a) 求 Bala 所得占总金额的几分之几，答案化为最简分数。

(b) Chen 分得 240 元。计算这笔钱的总金额以及 Amira 分得的金额。

Answer / 答案：

(a) 3/10 (b) Total = $1200; Amira receives $600

解析：

步骤1（求总份数）：5 + 3 + 2 = 10 份。

步骤2（(a) 求 Bala 所占分数）：

Bala 占 3 份，故占总额的 3/10 = 3/10（已是最简分数）。

步骤3（(b) 由 Chen 的份额求每份金额）：

Chen 占 2 份，对应 $240，所以每份 = 240 ÷ 2 = $120。

步骤4（求总额）：总额 = 10 份 × $120 = $1200。

步骤5（求 Amira 的金额）：Amira 占 5 份，5 × $120 = $600。

步骤6（检验）：600 + 360 + 240 = 1200 ✓，且 600 : 360 : 240 = 5 : 3 : 2 ✓。

Q3 [3 marks]

EN:

The total value of a company's shares increased by 12% in 2024. The value at the end of 2024 was $3 080 000.

(a) Calculate the value of the shares at the beginning of 2024.

(b) In 2025 the value decreased by 15%. Find the percentage decrease in the value of the shares over the two years, correct to 3 significant figures.

中文：

某公司股票总市值在 2024 年增长了 12%，2024 年末的市值为 3 080 000 元。

(a) 计算 2024 年初该股票的市值。

(b) 2025 年市值下降了 15%。求这两年市值总的下降百分比，答案保留 3 位有效数字。

Answer / 答案：

(a) $2 750 000 (b) 4.80%

解析：

步骤1（(a) 反求百分比 reverse percentage）：

设 2024 年初市值为 P。增长 12% 后为 P × 1.12。

P × 1.12 = 3 080 000

P = 3 080 000 ÷ 1.12 = $2 750 000

步骤2（(b) 求 2025 年末市值）：

3 080 000 × (1 − 15%) = 3 080 000 × 0.85 = $2 618 000

步骤3（求两年总变化率）：

总乘数 = 1.12 × 0.85 = 0.952

即相对于 2024 年初，2025 年末市值变为原来的 95.2%。

步骤4（求下降百分比）：

下降百分比 = (1 − 0.952) × 100% = 4.8%

保留 3 位有效数字为 4.80%。

Q4 [3 marks]

EN:

A delivery van travels a distance of 84 km in 1 hour 10 minutes.

(a) Calculate the average speed of the van in km/h.

(b) A cyclist travels at a steady speed of 6 m/s. Express the van's average speed from part (a) in m/s and hence state how many times faster the van is than the cyclist. Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.

中文：

一辆送货车用 1 小时 10 分钟行驶了 84 公里。

(a) 计算该车的平均速度，单位 km/h。

(b) 一名骑车人以 6 m/s 的恒定速度行驶。将 (a) 中货车的平均速度换算为 m/s，并由此说明货车速度是骑车人的多少倍，答案保留 3 位有效数字。

Answer / 答案：

(a) 72 km/h (b) 20 m/s; the van is 3.33 times as fast as the cyclist

解析：

步骤1（(a) 换算时间）：

1 小时 10 分钟 = 1 + 10/60 = 1 + 1/6 = 7/6 小时。

步骤2（求平均速度）：

平均速度 = 距离 ÷ 时间 = 84 ÷ (7/6) = 84 × 6/7 = 72 km/h。

步骤3（(b) 单位换算，km/h → m/s）：

1 km/h = 1000 m ÷ 3600 s = 5/18 m/s

72 km/h = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s

步骤4（求倍数）：

20 ÷ 6 = 3.333…

保留 3 位有效数字 = 3.33（倍）。

Q5 [3 marks]

EN:

(a) Expand and simplify (3x − 2)(2x + 5).

(b) Factorise completely 2x² − 18.

(c) Simplify (x² − 4) / (x² + 5x + 6), stating any restriction on the value of x.

中文：

(a) 展开并化简 (3x − 2)(2x + 5)。

(b) 将 2x² − 18 完全因式分解。

(c) 化简 (x² − 4) / (x² + 5x + 6)，并写出 x 的取值范围限制。

Answer / 答案：

(a) 6x² + 11x − 10

(b) 2(x + 3)(x − 3)

(c) (x − 2)/(x + 3), where x ≠ −3 and x ≠ −2

解析：

步骤1（(a) 逐项展开）：

(3x − 2)(2x + 5) = 3x × 2x + 3x × 5 + (−2) × 2x + (−2) × 5

= 6x² + 15x − 4x − 10

合并同类项：= 6x² + 11x − 10

步骤2（(b) 先提取公因数，再用平方差公式 a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b)）：

2x² − 18 = 2(x² − 9) = 2(x² − 3²) = 2(x + 3)(x − 3)

步骤3（(c) 分子分母分别因式分解）：

分子 x² − 4 = (x + 2)(x − 2)（平方差）

分母 x² + 5x + 6 = (x + 2)(x + 3)（十字相乘：2 + 3 = 5，2 × 3 = 6）

步骤4（约去公因式 (x + 2)）：

(x² − 4)/(x² + 5x + 6) = [(x + 2)(x − 2)]/[(x + 2)(x + 3)] = (x − 2)/(x + 3)

步骤5（写限制条件：分母不能为零）：

x + 3 ≠ 0 且 x + 2 ≠ 0，即 x ≠ −3 且 x ≠ −2。

Q6 [3 marks]

EN:

The graph of the quadratic function y = x² − 6x + 5 cuts the x-axis at the points A and B, and has a minimum point M.

(a) Find the coordinates of A, B and M.

(b) State the equation of the line of symmetry of the graph.

中文：

二次函数 y = x² − 6x + 5 的图像与 x 轴交于点 A 和 B，并有一个最低点 M。

(a) 求 A、B、M 三点的坐标。

(b) 写出该图像对称轴的方程。

Answer / 答案：

(a) A(1, 0), B(5, 0), M(3, −4) (b) x = 3

解析：

步骤1（(a) 求与 x 轴交点：令 y = 0）：

x² − 6x + 5 = 0

因式分解：(x − 1)(x − 5) = 0

所以 x = 1 或 x = 5，即 A(1, 0)、B(5, 0)。

步骤2（求最低点 M：用配方法 completing the square）：

x² − 6x + 5 = (x² − 6x + 9) − 9 + 5 = (x − 3)² − 4

即 y = (x − 3)² − 4。

步骤3（读出顶点）：因 x² 系数为正，图像开口向上，故顶点为最低点。

顶点 M(3, −4)。

步骤4（(b) 对称轴）：

对称轴过顶点且垂直于 x 轴，方程为 x = 3。

（检验：A(1, 0) 与 B(5, 0) 的中点为 ((1+5)/2, 0) = (3, 0)，确在 x = 3 上，符合对称性。）

Q10 [3 marks]

EN:

The sizes of the interior angles of an irregular polygon with n sides are in arithmetic progression, and the polygon has exactly one reflex interior angle.

(a) Find the largest possible value of n.

(b) State one property of the diagonals of a kite.

中文：

一个 n 边形所有内角的大小构成等差数列，且该多边形恰有一个内角为优角（reflex angle）。

(a) 求 n 的最大可能值。

(b) 写出筝形（kite）对角线的一个性质。

Answer / 答案：

(a) n = 4 (b) The diagonals of a kite intersect at right angles; and one diagonal bisects the other (the diagonal joining the two vertices where the equal sides meet is the perpendicular bisector of the other diagonal).

解析：

步骤1（(a) 分析 n 边形能有多少个优角）：

n 边形内角和 = (n − 2) × 180°，其余 (n − 1) 个内角都小于 180°。

因恰有一个内角 > 180°，故未达 180° 的内角最多 (n − 1) 个，即最多 (n − 1) × 180°。

步骤2（列出不等式并求解）：

(n − 2) × 180° > (n − 1) × 180°

两边同时除以 180°：(n − 2) > (n − 1)，即 −2 > −1，此式恒不成立。

再检验：若多边形有 2 个优角，则未达 180° 的内角为 (n − 2) 个：

(n − 2) × 180° > (n − 2) × 180°，不成立。

步骤3（逐一检验小 n，找含优角的可能情形）：

n = 3：内角和 180°，三个角都 < 180°，不可能有优角。

n = 4：内角和 360° = 3 × 120°；若一个内角为 200°，其余三角和为 160°，都可小于 180°（如 60°、50°、50°），可行，呈凹四边形（箭头形）。

n = 5：内角和 540° = 3 × 180°；若一个内角为 200°，其余四角和为 340°，平均 85°，可行但需逐式检验是否成等差。

步骤4（结论）：本小题所设条件（内角成等差数列）下，最简洁且自洽的情形是 n = 4，即最大可能值 n = 4（凹四边形，内角如 60°、50°、50°、200° 三分和 160° + 200° = 360°）。

步骤5（(b) 筝形对角线的性质）：

筝形是两组邻边分别相等的四边形。其对角线性质为：两条对角线互相垂直；其中一条对角线（连接两组等邻边公共顶点的那条）垂直平分另一条对角线；并且它平分筝形的一组对角。

Q11 [3 marks]

EN:

A tower stands vertically on horizontal ground. At a point A on the ground, 48 m from the foot of the tower, the angle of elevation of the top of the tower is 32°. The point B is on the same straight line as A and the foot of the tower, and is 20 m closer to the tower.

(a) Calculate the height of the tower.

(b) Calculate the angle of elevation of the top of the tower from B, giving your answer correct to 1 decimal place.

中文：

一座塔垂直立于水平地面上。在地面上距塔底 48 m 的 A 点测得塔顶的仰角为 32°。点 B 与 A、塔底在同一直线上，且比 A 点更靠近塔 20 m。

(a) 计算塔的高度。

(b) 计算在 B 点测得塔顶的仰角，答案保留 1 位小数。

Answer / 答案：

(a) 30.0 m (b) 56.3°

解析：

步骤1（(a) 画图并确立关系）：设塔高为 h，塔底为 F，则 AF = 48 m，∠FAB = 90°。

在直角三角形 AF T 中，tan 32° = 对边 ÷ 邻边 = h ÷ 48。

步骤2（代入求解）：

h = 48 × tan 32° = 48 × 0.624 869… = 29.993… m

保留 3 位有效数字：h = 30.0 m

步骤3（(b) 求 B 到塔底的水平距离）：

B 比 A 更靠近塔 20 m，故 BF = 48 − 20 = 28 m。

步骤4（求仰角）：设从 B 看塔顶的仰角为 θ，

tan θ = h ÷ BF = 30.0 ÷ 28 = 1.071 428…

步骤5（求角）：

θ = tan⁻¹(1.071 428…) = 46.97…°

用未取整的 h = 29.993 m 精确计算：tan θ = 29.993/28 = 1.071 179…，θ = 46.97°。

θ = tan⁻¹(29.993…/28) = tan⁻¹(1.071 179…) ≈ 47.0°

修正答案：(b) 47.0°（correct to 1 decimal place）

Q12 [3 marks]

EN:

A bag contains 5 red balls and 3 blue balls. Two balls are taken from the bag at random, one after the other, without replacement.

(a) Draw a tree diagram to show all the possible outcomes and their probabilities.

(b) Find the probability that the two balls taken are of different colours.

中文：

一个袋中有 5 个红球和 3 个蓝球。随机地从袋中连续取出两个球（不放回）。

(a) 画出树形图，表示所有可能结果及其概率。

(b) 求取出的两个球颜色不同的概率。

Answer / 答案：

(a) First draw: P(R) = 5/8, P(B) = 3/8. Second draw: P(R|R) = 4/7, P(B|R) = 3/7, P(R|B) = 5/7, P(B|B) = 2/7.

(b) 15/28

解析：

步骤1（(a) 第一次抽取）：袋中共 5 + 3 = 8 个球。

P(红) = 5/8，P(蓝) = 3/8。

步骤2（第二次抽取，不放回）：

Q18 [6 marks]

EN: A student investigates the reaction between sodium thiosulfate solution and dilute hydrochloric acid, observing a yellow precipitate of sulfur.

(b) State and explain the effect on the rate of increasing the temperature. [4]

(b) 说明升高温度对反应速率的影响并解释原因。[4]

Answer / 答案：

(a) Measure the time taken for the yellow precipitate (sulfur) to obscure a cross marked on paper beneath the flask; the rate is proportional to 1/time.

(b) Increasing temperature increases the rate of reaction, because particles gain more kinetic energy and move faster. More particles collide with energy greater than or equal to the activation energy, so more frequent successful collisions occur per unit time.

解析：

步骤1：(a) 用「固定量的硫沉淀使烧瓶下纸上的十字记号变模糊所需时间」来度量：时间越短，速率越快。可定义 rate ∝ 1/t。

步骤2：(b) 速率增大。

步骤3：原因分两层：① 温度升高使粒子平均动能增大、运动加快，碰撞频率略增；② 更关键的是超过活化能的粒子比例大幅增加（能量分布曲线上活化能右侧面积显著变大）。

步骤4：结论：单位时间内有效碰撞增多 → 反应速率加快。

Q16 [6 marks]

EN: The diagram shows a plant cell placed in pure water.

(a) Name the structure that is freely permeable and surrounds the plant cell. [1]

(b) Describe what happens to the cell after 30 minutes, and explain your answer in terms of water potential. [3]

(c) State what would happen if the same cell were placed in a concentrated sugar solution. [2]

(a) 命名包围植物细胞且具全透性的结构。[1]

(c) 说明若将该细胞置于浓糖溶液中会发生什么。[2]

Answer / 答案：

(a) Cell wall.

(b) The cell becomes turgid. Water enters the cell by osmosis because the water potential outside is higher than inside the cell. The vacuole swells and pushes the cytoplasm against the cell wall, creating turgor pressure.

(c) Water would leave the cell by osmosis, the vacuole would shrink and the cell would become plasmolysed (the cytoplasm pulls away from the cell wall).

解析：

步骤1：(a) 细胞壁是全透性的（允许水和溶质自由通过），位于细胞最外层。

步骤2：(b) 纯水的水势（water potential）高于细胞液的水势。

步骤4：液泡吸水膨大，将细胞质压向细胞壁，产生膨压（turgor pressure），细胞变得饱满坚挺（turgid）。

步骤5：(c) 浓糖溶液水势低于细胞液。

步骤6：水由细胞内向外渗透流失，原生质体收缩与细胞壁分离，发生质壁分离（plasmolysis）。

Q17 [6 marks]

EN: An experiment investigates the effect of pH on the activity of the enzyme amylase.

(a) State the independent and dependent variables. [2]

(b) Predict and explain the effect of very low pH on enzyme activity. [3]

(c) State one other factor that should be kept constant. [1]

中文： 某实验研究 pH 对淀粉酶活性的影响。

(a) 指出自变量与因变量。[2]

(b) 预测并解释极低 pH 对酶活性的影响。[3]

(c) 说明另一个应保持恒定的因素。[1]

Answer / 答案：

(a) Independent variable: pH. Dependent variable: rate of enzyme activity (e.g. time for starch to be broken down).

(b) At very low pH, enzyme activity decreases (or stops). The enzyme's active site changes shape (denaturation) because the extreme pH breaks the bonds maintaining the enzyme's 3D structure, so the substrate no longer fits.

(c) Temperature (or substrate concentration / enzyme concentration / volume).

解析：

步骤1：(a) 自变量：pH（人为改变的变量）；因变量：酶活性（例如淀粉分解所需时间，或碘液褪色时间）。

步骤2：(b) 极低 pH 会破坏酶的三维结构（使维持结构的氢键、离子键断裂）。

步骤3：活性位点（active site）变形 → 底物无法与之契合 → 催化能力丧失。

步骤4：此过程称变性（denaturation），通常是不可逆的。

步骤5：(c) 需恒定因素：温度（或底物浓度、酶浓度、体积等）——保证单一变量。

Section A: Situational Writing 情境写作

Task 1 [30 marks]

EN: Your school's library has been closed for renovation for three months. Many students are unhappy because they have nowhere quiet to study. You are the President of the Student Council. Write a letter to the Principal to propose opening a temporary study room in the school hall. In your letter:

  • Explain why students need a quiet study space
  • Describe the problems caused by the library closure
  • Propose your solution and explain how it could work

Write 250–350 words. You may use your own name and school name.

中文： 你校图书馆因翻修关闭了三个月。许多学生不满，因为没有安静的地方学习。你是学生会主席。写一封信给校长，提议在学校礼堂开设临时自习室。信中须：

  • 说明学生为何需要安静的学习空间
  • 描述图书馆关闭造成的问题
  • 提出你的解决方案并解释其可行性

字数 250–350 字。可使用自己的姓名与校名。

Model Answer / 范文：

Dear Mr Tan,

I am writing on behalf of the Student Council to propose a solution to a problem that has been troubling many of our schoolmates — the lack of a quiet study space since the library closed for renovation three months ago.

The response from students has been overwhelming. In a recent survey of 200 students, 87% said they had been unable to find a suitable place to revise for their O-Level examinations. Those who attempt to study in the canteen are constantly interrupted by noise, while those who remain in classrooms after school are often asked to leave by teachers preparing for the next day's lessons.

The consequences are serious. Several students have told me they now study late into the night at home simply because they cannot concentrate in school, which has left many of them exhausted during lessons. With the examination period approaching, this is a situation we cannot afford to ignore.

I would therefore like to propose that the school hall be converted into a temporary study room. It is large enough to accommodate over 100 students, and its high ceilings and thick walls mean it is naturally quieter than most other venues in the school. With a small number of tables drawn from the canteen and a rota of student volunteers to maintain silence, the hall could be put to good use at very little cost.

We hope you will consider this proposal favourably. I would be glad to present a more detailed plan at your convenience.

Yours sincerely,

Lim Wei Jia

President, Student Council

Task 2 [30 marks]

EN: Your school is organising a "Green Week" to encourage students to protect the environment. As the head of the Environment Club, write a report for the Principal describing what activities were organised during Green Week, and evaluating how successful they were. Include recommendations for next year.

Write 250–350 words.

中文： 你校正在组织"绿色周"，鼓励学生保护环境。作为环保社团负责人，写一份报告给校长，描述绿色周期间组织的活动并评估其成效，并附上来年的建议。

字数 250–350 字。

Model Answer / 范文：

REPORT ON GREEN WEEK 2026

Introduction

Green Week was held from 5 to 9 May 2026. This report describes the activities organised by the Environment Club and evaluates their success.

Activities Organised

The week began with a "No Plastic Day", during which canteen vendors agreed to use only biodegradable containers. On Wednesday, a guest speaker from a local conservation group delivered a talk on recycling to all Secondary Three students. On Friday, volunteers led a clean-up of the nearby park, during which over 60 bags of litter were collected.

Evaluation

Overall, the week was a success. Participation exceeded expectations, with over 400 students taking part in at least one activity. The "No Plastic Day" was particularly effective: canteen waste dropped by approximately 40% compared with a normal day. However, the guest talk was less successful, as it was held in the afternoon and many students were tired after a full day of lessons.

Recommendations

It is recommended that next year's Green Week include a competition between classes to encourage wider participation. The guest talk should be scheduled in the morning to ensure students are more alert.

Conclusion

Green Week raised environmental awareness across the school. With the improvements suggested above, next year's event should be even more effective.

Reported by: Chen Hui Min, Head of Environment Club

Section B: Continuous Writing 连续写作

Task 3 [30 marks]

EN: Write a story which begins with the words: "The door creaked open and I froze."

Write 350–500 words.

中文： 写一篇以 "门吱呀一声开了，我僵住了。" 开头的故事。

字数 350–500 字。

Model Answer / 范文（开头与主体节选）：

The door creaked open and I froze. My torch flickered weakly in my trembling hand, casting restless shadows across the walls of the abandoned warehouse. I had only wanted to retrieve the drone I had crashed through the skylight the day before — a foolish accident I was desperate to hide from my parents. Now, standing in the darkness, I wished I had simply confessed.

I held my breath. For a long moment, there was only the drumming of my own heart. Then I heard it again: a soft, scuffing sound, like shoes dragging across concrete. Someone — or something — was moving inside.

My first instinct was to run. But my legs refused to obey. Instead, I pressed myself against the cold brick wall and strained my eyes into the gloom. A pale sliver of moonlight fell through the broken skylight, and in it I saw a small, hunched figure crouched beside an old crate. It was a boy, perhaps no older than ten, wrapped in a jacket far too large for him.

"You scared me," I whispered, my voice cracking.

He looked up. His face, thin and smudged with dirt, wore an expression not of menace but of fear. "I'm hiding," he said simply. "From them."

It took me a moment to understand. Then I heard the shouting from outside — coarse, angry voices calling a name. The boy flinched. In that instant, my own small errand seemed impossibly trivial.

I do not know what possessed me. Perhaps it was the way he looked at me, as though I were the only person in the world who might help. I pulled off my jacket and draped it over his shoulders, and together we slipped out through a gap in the back wall, unseen.

We never spoke again after that night. But I have never forgotten the lesson that child taught me in that dark, dusty warehouse: that courage is not the absence of fear, but the decision, made in a single trembling moment, to help someone else.

中文范文翻译（对应节选）：

门吱呀一声开了，我僵住了。手电筒在我颤抖的手中微弱地闪烁，在废弃仓库的墙壁上投下不安的阴影。我本只是想取回前一天从气窗坠落的无人机——一个我急着向父母隐瞒的愚蠢意外。此刻，站在黑暗中，我真希望自己当时干脆坦白。

我屏住呼吸。好一会儿，只有自己心跳的鼓动声。接着我又听到了：一阵轻微的摩擦声，像鞋子拖过水泥地。有人——或什么东西——正在里面移动。

我的第一反应是跑。可我的双腿不听使唤。我紧贴冰冷的砖墙，努力向黑暗中张望。一缕苍白的月光从破损的气窗洒下，在那光里，我看见一个瘦小蜷缩的身影蹲在旧木箱旁。是个男孩，也许不超过十岁，裹着一件对他而言太大的夹克。

"你吓到我了。"我低声说，声音发颤。

他抬起头。那张消瘦、沾满尘土的脸上，不是威胁，而是恐惧。"我在躲。"他简单地说，"躲他们。"

我花了片刻才明白。随后我听到外面的喊声——粗野愤怒的声音在喊一个名字。男孩瑟缩了一下。那一瞬间，我自己那桩小事显得无比微不足道。

我不知道是什么驱使了我。也许是他看我的眼神，仿佛我是这世上唯一可能帮他的人。我脱下夹克披在他肩上，我们一起从后墙的缺口溜了出去，无人察觉。

那夜之后我们再未说过话。但我永远忘不了那个孩子在那间昏暗多尘的仓库里教会我的道理：勇气并非没有恐惧，而是在一个颤抖的瞬间，做出帮助他人的决定。

  • 开头衔接（Opening）：严格以给定句子开头，自然过渡。
  • 情节结构（Plot）：有铺垫（setting/purpose）、冲突（clash）、高潮（climax）、转折与结尾（resolution）。
  • 感官描写（Sensory Details）：视觉、听觉、触觉描写增强代入感。

Task 4 [30 marks]

EN: "School uniforms should be abolished." Do you agree?

Write 350–500 words, giving reasons and examples to support your view.

中文： "校服应被废除。"你是否同意？

字数 350–500 字，给出理由与例证支持你的观点。

Model Answer / 范文：

Every morning, millions of students around the world face the same routine: buttoning a stiff collar, tugging at a tie, and checking their reflection in a uniform that leaves little room for individuality. In recent years, growing numbers of students and parents have argued that school uniforms should be abolished. While the case for doing so is not entirely without merit, I believe uniforms serve purposes that outweigh their drawbacks.

The strongest argument against uniforms is that they suppress self-expression. During adolescence, young people are discovering who they are, and clothing is one of the most natural outlets for that discovery. Being told to dress identically every day can feel stifling. Furthermore, uniforms can be expensive: a full set may cost several hundred dollars, a genuine burden for low-income families.

However, the arguments in favour of uniforms are, in my view, more compelling. First, uniforms promote equality. In schools without them, wealthier students often wear designer brands while poorer students feel embarrassed by their clothing, creating quiet but real divides. A uniform removes this source of anxiety entirely. Second, uniforms foster a sense of belonging. Wearing the same colours as one's classmates creates a shared identity that strengthens school spirit and reduces bullying based on appearance.

A third consideration is often overlooked: uniforms save time. Students need not agonise over what to wear each morning, freeing mental energy for more important matters — their studies. While some students may object that uniforms are uncomfortable or unattractive, modern designs increasingly address these concerns, and no uniform is so unpleasant that it justifies abandoning the benefits altogether.

In conclusion, although uniforms do restrict self-expression and can be costly, their advantages — equality, belonging and simplicity — are far more significant. Rather than abolishing uniforms, schools should improve them: make them more affordable, more comfortable and perhaps more varied. In doing so, we preserve what works while remedying what does not.

中文范文翻译：

每天早晨，全世界数百万学生都面对着相同的流程：扣上僵硬的衣领，拉好领带，在镜中打量那身几乎不给个性留空间的校服。近年来，越来越多学生与家长主张废除校服。尽管这一主张并非全无道理，但我认为校服所起的作用超过了它的弊端。

反对校服最有力的理由是它压制了自我表达。在青春期，年轻人正在探索自我，而衣着是这种探索最自然的出口之一。被要求每天穿着相同，会令人感到窒息。此外，校服可能很贵：一整套可能花费数百元，对低收入家庭是实实在在的负担。

然而在我看来，支持校服的理由更有说服力。首先，校服促进平等。在没有校服的学校，富裕学生常穿名牌，而较贫困的学生因衣着感到窘迫，制造出无声却真实的分化。校服完全消除了这一焦虑来源。其次，校服培养归属感。与同学穿同样的颜色，创造出共同的认同，增强学校精神，减少因外表产生的欺凌。

第三点常被忽视：校服节省时间。学生不必每天早晨为穿什么而纠结，把精力留给更重要的事——学业。虽然有些学生可能抱怨校服不舒适或不好看，但现代设计越来越能解决这些问题，也没有哪套校服难看到足以让人放弃其全部好处。

总之，尽管校服确实限制自我表达且可能昂贵，但它的优势——平等、归属与简洁——要重要得多。与其废除校服，不如改进它：让它更便宜、更舒适、也许更多样。如此，我们既保留有效之处，又纠正不足。

评分要点 / Marking Points：

  • 立场明确（Clear Thesis）：开头即表明观点（部分反对废除，主张改进）。
  • 论证结构（Argumentation）：承认反方合理之处（concession），再给出压倒性论证。
  • 论据充分（Evidence）：平等、归属、节省时间三条理由 + 具体例证。
  • 反驳与平衡（Counter-argument）：正面回应"压抑个性""昂贵""不舒适"等反对意见。
  • 结论有力（Conclusion）：提出"改进而非废除"的折中方案。
  • 语言（Language）：衔接词（First / Second / Furthermore / However / In conclusion）、正式议论文语体。

Paper 2 阅读理解与语言运用（Comprehension & Language Use）

Passage A

EN (Reading Passage):

The old man had been repairing shoes on the same corner of Serangoon Road for forty years. His name was Mr Krishnan, and although few of his customers knew it, he had once been a teacher. A single mistake in his youth — a decision he still could not bring himself to describe — had cost him the profession he loved, and he had rebuilt his life one stitch at a time.

His stall was little more than a wooden box and a canvas awning, yet to the residents of the neighbourhood it was something more. Here, in the shade of the awning, people brought not only their broken sandals but also their troubles. Mr Krishnan listened with the patience of a man who had learned that silence often heals more than advice. He never charged a schoolchild, and he never forgot a face.

One afternoon, a young boy arrived with a pair of school shoes so worn that the soles flapped like tired wings. The boy's mother stood behind him, apologetic, explaining that she could not afford new shoes before the new term. Mr Krishnan examined the shoes slowly, turning them over in his weathered hands. Then he looked up and smiled. "Come back tomorrow," he said.

When the boy returned, the shoes were transformed. Not merely mended — the soles had been replaced and polished, and a small, neat stitch in the shape of a star adorned each heel. "Why a star?" the boy asked. Mr Krishnan laughed softly. "So that you will always remember," he said, "that even broken things can shine."

Years later, that boy, now a doctor, would walk past the corner where the stall had once stood. The old man had long since passed away, and a glass-fronted shop had taken his place. But every time he saw a child in worn shoes, the doctor would remember the star — and the lesson that kindness, quietly given, outlasts almost everything else.

中文（阅读短文翻译）：

这位老人已在实龙岗路的同一个角落修了四十年的鞋。他叫克里斯南先生，尽管少有顾客知道，他曾是一名教师。年轻时的一个错误——一个他至今仍不忍提起的决定——使他失去了所热爱的职业，他靠一针一线重建了自己的人生。

他的摊位不过是一个木箱加一块帆布遮阳篷，但对街坊而言，它不止于此。在这遮阳篷的荫下，人们带来的不只是坏掉的凉鞋，还有各自的烦恼。克里斯南先生以一种饱经世故的耐心倾听——他早已懂得，沉默往往比建议更能疗愈。他从不向学生收费，也从不忘记一张面孔。

一天下午，一个小男孩拿来一双校鞋，破旧得鞋底像疲惫的翅膀一样翻翘。男孩的母亲站在他身后，满怀歉意地解释，新学年之前她买不起新鞋。克里斯南先生慢慢地端详那双鞋，在他布满风霜的手中翻来覆去。然后他抬起头，笑了。"明天再来。"他说。

男孩回来时，鞋子已经焕然一新。不只是修补——鞋底被换掉并擦亮，每只鞋跟上还缀着一道工整的、星形的缝线。"为什么是星星？"男孩问。克里斯南先生轻声笑了。"这样你就会永远记得，"他说，"即使破碎之物，也能发光。"

多年以后，那个男孩——如今已是一名医生——走过那摊位曾矗立的角落。老人早已过世，一家玻璃门面的店铺取代了它。但每当他看到一个穿着旧鞋的孩子，这位医生便会想起那颗星星——以及那个道理：悄然给予的善意，比几乎一切都更长久。

Q1 [2 marks]

EN: From the first paragraph, what was Mr Krishnan's profession before he became a shoe repairer?

中文： 从第一段可知，克里斯南先生在成为修鞋匠之前的职业是什么？

Answer / 答案： He had once been a teacher.

解析： 定位第一段第二句："although few of his customers knew it, he had once been a teacher"。答案直接可查，属字面理解题，直接引用原文作答即可。注意用完整句回答，不必照抄整句。

Q2 [3 marks]

EN: Using your own words, explain what the writer means by "silence often heals more than advice" (paragraph 2).

中文： 用自己的话解释作者在第二段所说的"沉默往往比建议更能疗愈"的含义。

Answer / 答案： Mr Krishnan listened patiently without interrupting or telling people what to do, because he had learned that simply being heard can comfort people more than giving them advice does.

解析：

步骤1：定位原句上下文——"Mr Krishnan listened with the patience of a man who had learned that silence often heals more than advice."

步骤2：拆解含义：他倾听而不打断、不指导。

步骤3：因为"被倾听"本身比"被建议"更能抚慰人心。

步骤4：改写要求：不能照抄原句，须换成自己的表达（listening without interrupting / being heard comforts people）。

步骤5：这类题考查 paraphrase 能力，直接用原文词汇会失分。

考点 / Topic: 阅读理解（改写理解 / inference & paraphrase）

Q3 [3 marks]

EN: Why did Mr Krishnan put a star-shaped stitch on each heel? Give two reasons from the passage.

中文： 克里斯南先生为什么在每只鞋跟上缝一个星形？根据短文给出两个原因。

Answer / 答案：

(1) So that the boy would always remember the lesson that even broken things can shine;

(2) To give the boy hope and joy — the gesture shows his kindness towards the child.

Q1(b) [5 marks]

Answer / 答案：

Car parks are covered with dark, impervious surfaces such as asphalt or tarmac, which absorb and store large amounts of solar radiation during the day. Answer / 答案：

In the middle course, the river flows over gentler gradients, so lateral (sideways) erosion becomes dominant. On the outer bend, the faster-flowing water has more energy and erodes the bank through hydraulic action and abrasion, forming a river cliff. 步骤2：应对手段：高强度用地（高楼、集約）与填海造地（成本高）。

步骤3：挑战二：资源依赖外部——粮食、水、能源高度依赖进口。

步骤4：后果：供应链脆弱（易受全球市场波动与地缘风险影响）。

步骤5：答题须点出"空间"与"资源"两条主线。

Q3(b) [5 marks]

EN: Explain how Singapore has increased its water supply security.

中文： 解释新加坡如何提升其水资源供应安全。

Answer / 答案：

Singapore uses the "Four National Taps" strategy: (1) importing water from Malaysia; (2) collecting and storing rainwater in reservoirs and urban catchment areas; (3) recycling used water into NEWater, which is ultra-clean and used in industry and returned to reservoirs; and (4) desalinating seawater. Together these diversify sources, reduce dependence on any single supplier and build resilience against drought and supply disruption.

解析：

步骤1：四大水龙头（Four National Taps） 策略：

① 进口水——从马来西亚购入（逐步降低依赖）；

② 集雨水——水库与城市集水区收集降雨；

③ 新生水（NEWater）——污水深度处理回用；

④ 海水淡化——反渗透技术。

步骤2：多样化来源 → 降低单一依赖 → 提升韧性（resilience）。

步骤3：配套：节水政策、水价调节、公众教育。

步骤4：答题须列举多种水源并说明多样化的作用机制。

Q3(c) [4 marks]

EN: Describe two ways Singapore manages its waste, and explain why these are important for a small country.

中文： 描述新加坡管理废弃物的两种方式，并解释为何对小国而言这些很重要。

Answer / 答案：

(1) Incineration with energy recovery. Waste is burned in waste-to-energy plants, reducing its volume by about 90% and generating electricity. This is vital because land for landfills is extremely scarce.

(2) Recycling (e.g. the "3R" approach). Materials such as paper, plastic and metal are collected and reprocessed. This reduces the volume of waste needing disposal and conserves resources that Singapore cannot produce itself.

解析：

步骤1：方式一：焚化 + 能源回收——体积减少约 90%，余热发电。

步骤2：重要性：土地极度稀缺（唯一垃圾填埋场实马高岛有限），焚化可大幅延长填埋场寿命。

步骤3：方式二：回收利用（3R）——减少待处理垃圾量，节约本国无法自产的资源。

步骤4：可补充：实马高岛（Semakau）填埋场、包装减量公约。

步骤5：答题须"方式 + 对小国的意义"配对。

Paper 1 史料题（Source-Based Case Study）

Case Study A: Decolonisation in Southeast Asia [25 marks]

EN (Context): The following sources relate to the decolonisation of Southeast Asia after the Second World War.

中文（情境）： 以下史料涉及二战后东南亚的去殖民化进程。

Source A: An extract from a speech made by a nationalist leader in Malaya in 1952.

"For too long, we have been told what to do by people who live ten thousand miles away. They do not know our villages, they do not speak our languages, and they do not share our customs. We do not ask for violence. We ask only for the right to govern ourselves, to decide our own future, and to stand as equals among the nations of the world."

Source B: An extract from a British government report on Malaya, written in 1953.

"The Emergency has shown that a hasty withdrawal would leave a dangerous power vacuum. Our policy must therefore be gradual. We shall build up local institutions, train local administrators, and transfer power only when stability is assured. The security of trade routes and our economic interests must also be safeguarded during any transition."

Source C: An extract from a newspaper article in Singapore, 1957.

"Independence has come not through the barrel of a gun but through patient negotiation. Yet the celebrations in the streets hide a deeper anxiety: can a new nation, its treasury nearly empty and its people divided by language and loyalty, truly stand on its own? The answer will be written in the years to come."

中文（史料翻译）：

史料 A： 摘自马来亚一位民族主义领袖 1952 年的演讲。

"长久以来，我们一直由住在万里之外的人指手画脚。他们不了解我们的村庄，不会说我们的语言，也不认同我们的习俗。我们不求暴力。我们只求自治的权利，自决未来的权利，以及作为平等一员立于世界民族之林的权利。"

史料 B： 摘自英国政府 1953 年关于马来亚的报告。

"紧急状态表明，仓促撤出会留下危险的权力真空。因此我们的政策必须是渐进的。我们将建立本地机构、培训本地行政人员，只有在稳定得到保障时才移交权力。过渡期间也必须维护贸易通道的安全与我们的经济利益。"

史料 C： 摘自新加坡 1957 年的一篇报纸文章。

"独立并非靠枪杆子取得，而是靠耐心的谈判。然而街头的庆祝掩盖着更深的忧虑：一个国库几近空虚、人民被语言与忠诚所分裂的新生国家，真能自立吗？答案将在未来数年写就。"

Q1(a) [5 marks]

EN: What can you learn from Source A about the reasons for the nationalist leader's demand for self-government? Use details from the source to support your answer.

中文： 从史料 A 中可以了解这位民族主义领袖要求自治的哪些原因？请用史料中的细节支持你的答案。

Answer / 答案：

From Source A I can learn that the nationalist leader resented being ruled by distant foreigners who "live ten thousand miles away" and who did not understand local life — they "do not know our villages", "do not speak our languages" and "do not share our customs". This suggests the demand for self-government arose from a rejection of foreign rule that lacked local understanding. He also demanded "the right to govern ourselves" and to "decide our own future", implying a desire for national dignity and equality among nations, and he emphasised "we do not ask for violence", showing the movement sought peaceful change.

解析：

步骤1：本题要求从单一史料中提取信息（inference from source）。

步骤2：第一层含义：外来统治者不了解本地——用"万里之外""不知村庄""不说语言""不认同习俗"等细节支撑。

步骤3：第二层含义：诉求核心是自治与自决，追求民族尊严与国际平等。

步骤4：第三层含义：明确"不求暴力"→ 主张和平变革。

步骤5：答题要点：必须引用史料原句支撑，先"学到什么"再"证据是什么"。

步骤6：常见失分：只抄史料不作推断，或凭背景知识作答而不用史料。

考点 / Topic: 史料推断（inference from a single source）

Q1(b) [7 marks]

EN: Compare Source A and Source B. How far do the two sources differ in their attitudes towards the transfer of power in Malaya? Explain your answer using details from both sources.

中文： 比较史料 A 与史料 B。两则史料对马来亚权力移交的态度有多大差异？请用两则史料的细节解释。

Answer / 答案：

The two sources differ significantly. Source A (a nationalist leader) demands immediate self-government, framing it as a right and rejecting foreign rule as ignorant of local life — he wants power transferred because it is just. Source B (the British government) does not oppose transfer but insists it must be "gradual", with power handed over only "when stability is assured", and it also seeks to protect "trade routes" and "economic interests".

However, they are not entirely opposed. Both, in different ways, accept that power will eventually be transferred — Source A demands it, Source B plans for it. Source B's emphasis on training "local administrators" suggests an acknowledgement that self-rule is coming, which resembles Source A's goal of self-government, even if the timing differs.

The main difference is therefore one of timing and motivation: Source A wants self-rule now and on principle; Source B wants it later and on condition of stability and continued economic security.

解析：

步骤1：比较题（comparison） 需从态度差异入手，并双向引用。

步骤2：史料 A 态度：要求立即自治，视为天赋权利，否定外来统治的正当性（动机：正义）。

步骤3：史料 B 态度：不反对移交，但强调渐进、稳定为前提，并要保护经济与贸易利益（动机：安全与利益）。

步骤4：关键得分点：指出两者并非完全对立——都承认最终会移交权力。

步骤5：归纳核心差异：时机（立即 vs 渐进） 与 动机（权利 vs 稳定/利益）。

步骤6：答题须"差异 + 相似 + 归纳"三段式。

考点 / Topic: 史料比较（comparison of sources）

Q1(c) [7 marks]

EN: How useful is Source C to a historian studying the challenges faced by newly independent nations in Southeast Asia? Explain your answer.

中文： 对于研究东南亚新独立国家所面临挑战的历史学家而言，史料 C 有多大用处？请解释你的答案。

Answer / 答案：

Source C is useful in several ways. It is from a newspaper published in 1957 — close in time to the independence events it describes — and it directly identifies key challenges: an "empty treasury" (economic weakness), a people "divided by language and loyalty" (social and political division), and the anxiety over whether the nation can "stand on its own" (survival and self-reliance). It also reveals that independence was achieved by "patient negotiation" rather than violence, which is valuable evidence about how decolonisation occurred.

However, its usefulness is limited. As a newspaper article, it reflects the opinions of one publication in one city, so it may not represent views across the wider region. It is also an immediate contemporary account, written before the outcomes were known — its prediction that "the answer will be written in the years to come" shows it could not evaluate long-term consequences. A historian would therefore need to corroborate it with government records, economic data and accounts from other countries.

Overall, Source C is useful as contemporary evidence of the anxieties at independence and of the peaceful path taken, but it must be used alongside other sources to reach balanced conclusions.

解析：

步骤1：有用性评价题（usefulness） 需分析内容价值 + 来源局限两面。

步骤2：有用之处：

① 时代接近（1957 年，事件同期）；

② 直接点出经济困难（国库空虚）、社会分裂（语言与忠诚）、生存焦虑；

③ 揭示独立路径（耐心谈判而非暴力）。

步骤3：局限之处：

① 单一报刊，观点代表性有限；

② 当时视角，无法评价长期后果（原文自称"答案将在未来写就"）。

步骤4：方法论要求：需与其他史料交叉印证（corroborate）——如政府档案、经济数据、他国记录。

步骤5：给出总结判断，不可只列优缺点。

步骤6：常见失分：只讲内容不讲来源性质；未提 cross-reference。

Case Study B: The Cold War in Asia [25 marks]

EN (Context): The following sources relate to the spread of the Cold War into Asia during the 1950s and 1960s.

中文（情境）： 以下史料涉及 1950 至 1960 年代冷战在亚洲的扩展。

Source A: An extract from a speech by a United States Secretary of State, 1954.

"If we permit the nations of Asia to fall one by one like dominoes, the consequences will be grave. The fall of one to communist control will lead inevitably to pressure on its neighbours, and so on until the entire region is endangered. We must therefore commit ourselves to the defence of free nations wherever they stand."

Source B: An extract from a statement by a newly independent Asian state, 1961.

"We are weary of being asked to choose between two great powers. Our nation was not consulted when the world was divided. We seek neither to be a satellite of the East nor a puppet of the West. Our security lies in refusing to take sides and in working with all nations that respect our sovereignty."

Source C: An extract from a historian's account, published 1998.

"The Cold War in Asia was not simply a contest imposed from outside. Local leaders frequently used the rivalry between the superpowers to advance their own domestic agendas, obtaining weapons, aid and political backing in exchange for alignment. To view Asian nations merely as passive victims of the Cold War is therefore to misunderstand the period."

中文（史料翻译）：

史料 A： 摘自美国国务卿 1954 年的一次演讲。

"如果我们任由亚洲各国像多米诺骨牌一样逐一倒下，后果将十分严重。一国落入共产党控制，必将对其邻国施加压力，如此下去直到整个地区都陷入危境。因此我们必须致力于保卫自由国家，无论它们在何处。"

史料 B： 摘自某新独立的亚洲国家 1961 年的声明。

"我们厌倦了被要求在两个大国之间选边站。世界被分裂时，并没有征询过我们的意见。我们既不想做东方的卫星国，也不愿做西方的傀儡。我们的安全在于拒绝站队，并与所有尊重我们主权的国家合作。"

史料 C： 摘自一位历史学家 1998 年的论述。

"亚洲的冷战并非单纯由外部强加的争夺。本地领导人常常利用超级大国之间的对抗来推进自身的国内议程，以结盟换取武器、援助和政治支持。因此，把亚洲国家仅仅视为冷战的被动受害者，是对那个时期的误解。"

Q2(a) [5 marks]

EN: What is the message of Source A? Explain your answer using details from the source.

中文： 史料 A 传达了什么信息？请用史料中的细节解释。

Answer / 答案：

The message of Source A is that the United States believed it was necessary to intervene in Asia to prevent the spread of communism. The author uses the "domino" analogy — "fall one by one like dominoes" — to argue that if one nation fell to communist control, its neighbours would be threatened in turn, until the "entire region is endangered". Therefore, he argues, the US "must commit ourselves to the defence of free nations wherever they stand". The message is a justification for active American involvement in Asia as a defensive necessity.

解析：

步骤1："message"（主旨信息） 题要求提炼作者的核心主张，而非复述内容。

步骤2：核心主张：美国必须介入亚洲以遏制共产主义扩散。

步骤3：论证手法：多米诺骨牌比喻——一国倒下将连锁威胁邻国直至整个地区。

步骤4：结论：美国应保卫一切"自由国家" → 为主动介入提供正当性。

步骤5：答题须"主张 + 手法 + 证据"三部分。

步骤6：常见失分：把 message 写成 summary（只叙述不提炼观点）。

考点 / Topic: 史料主旨提炼（message）

Q2(b) [7 marks]

EN: Why do Source A and Source B differ in their views on the Cold War? Explain your answer using details from both sources and your own knowledge.

中文： 为什么史料 A 与史料 B 对冷战的看法不同？请用两则史料的细节及你自己的知识解释。

Answer / 答案：

The sources differ because of the different origins, interests and positions of their authors.

Source A comes from a United States Secretary of State in 1954 — a representative of a superpower deeply involved in the Cold War. From America's perspective, the spread of communism was an existential threat, so its view is global and strategic: it sees Asia as a battleground where "free nations" must be defended to prevent a domino effect.

Source B comes from a newly independent Asian state in 1961 — a nation with no superpower ambitions, whose priority was survival and sovereignty. Its view is that of a country that was "not consulted when the world was divided", and which therefore resists being made a "satellite" or "puppet". Its interest lies in non-alignment, not in winning the Cold War.

The difference is therefore shaped by perspective and national interest: a superpower sees the Cold War as a contest to be won, whereas a small newly independent state sees it as a rivalry to be avoided. Their differing geopolitical positions, and the different pressures they faced, explain their contrasting attitudes.

解析：

步骤1："Why differ"题 必须从来源背景（origin） 入手，即"谁说的、什么立场、为何这样说"。

步骤2：史料 A 来源：美国国务卿（1954）——超级大国代表，视共产主义为生存威胁。

步骤3：A 视角：全球战略——亚洲是"自由国家"必须防守的战场（多米诺逻辑）。

步骤5：B 视角：不结盟——拒绝成为"卫星国"或"傀儡"。

步骤6：归纳原因：立场与利益不同——大国视其为"必须赢的争夺"，小国视其为"必须避开的对抗"。

Q2(c) [7 marks]

EN: "Local leaders in Asia used the Cold War to benefit themselves rather than being its victims." How far do Sources A, B and C support this view? Explain your answer using all three sources.

中文： "亚洲本地领导人利用冷战为自身谋利，而非冷战的受害者。"史料 A、B、C 在多大程度上支持这一观点？请用三则史料解释。

Answer / 答案：

Source C strongly supports the view. It argues explicitly that Asian leaders "used the rivalry between the superpowers to advance their own domestic agendas", obtaining "weapons, aid and political backing in exchange for alignment", and it criticises the idea that Asian nations were merely "passive victims".

Source A partly supports the view indirectly. It shows that the United States was willing to offer defence and support to "free nations" that aligned with it — implying that Asian leaders who aligned could obtain American backing, which they could then use for their own purposes. However, Source A itself depicts Asian nations as potential "dominoes" — objects of superpower contest — which does not support the view.

Source B does not support the view. It presents an Asian state determined to avoid both sides and prioritising "sovereignty", portraying itself as resisting superpower pressure rather than exploiting it. This is closer to the "victim" or at least "reluctant participant" interpretation.

Overall, the sources give partial support: Source C directly endorses the view, Source A offers circumstantial support, while Source B contradicts it. The view therefore has merit but cannot be fully sustained by these sources alone.

步骤1："How far do sources support"题 须逐则分析并给出总结判断。

步骤2：史料 C：强烈支持——明确提出本地领导人"利用对抗推进国内议程"、以结盟换援助。

步骤3：史料 A：间接支持——美国愿意支持结盟的"自由国家"，暗示结盟者可获援助；但 A 本身把亚洲国家描述为"多米诺骨牌"（被动对象），不支持该观点。

步骤4：史料 B：不支持——强调拒绝站队、优先主权，更接近"受害者/被迫参与者"。

Question 1 [15 marks]

EN: Explain how the Second World War contributed to the rise of nationalism in Southeast Asia.

中文： 解释第二次世界大战如何促成东南亚民族主义的兴起。

Answer / 答案（参考答案框架）：

Introduction: The Second World War weakened colonial powers and changed local attitudes, contributing significantly to the rise of nationalism in Southeast Asia.

Point 1 — The defeat of colonial powers destroyed their image of invincibility. European powers such as Britain and the Netherlands were humiliated by Japan's rapid conquests in 1941–42, which shattered the belief that colonial rulers were superior or permanent. Local populations witnessed Asians defeating Europeans, undermining the psychological basis of colonial rule.

Point 2 — Japanese occupation created new political opportunities. The Japanese encouraged anti-Western sentiment and permitted limited local participation in administration, inadvertently giving nationalist leaders experience in organisation and governance. Some leaders obtained military training and political platforms they would later use against the returning colonial powers.

Point 3 — Economic hardship fuelled resentment. The war caused shortages, inflation and forced labour, which increased popular discontent with foreign control and strengthened demands for self-rule.

Point 4 — The post-war international climate supported self-determination. The founding of the United Nations and the Atlantic Charter's principle of self-determination provided moral and diplomatic support for nationalist movements, making continued colonial rule increasingly difficult to justify.

Conclusion: The war was a major turning point — it destroyed colonial prestige, created political opportunities, deepened economic grievances and reshaped international norms, together producing a powerful surge of nationalism across Southeast Asia.

中文解析：

步骤1：论述题结构：引言（立场）+ 分论点（每点"主张 + 解释 + 例证"）+ 结论。

步骤2：论点一：殖民者形象崩塌——日本 1941–42 年迅速击败英、荷，打破"欧洲人不可战胜"的迷思。

步骤3：论点二：日本占领创造政治机会——煽动反西方情绪、有限授权本地人参与行政、提供军事训练。

步骤4：论点三：经济苦难激化不满——物资短缺、通胀、强制劳役。

步骤5：论点四：战后国际环境支持自决——联合国成立、《大西洋宪章》自决原则。

步骤6：结论：战争是转折点——摧毁威望 + 创造机会 + 加深民怨 + 重塑规范。

步骤7：评分要点：因果解释（explain how）而非单纯叙述；每个论点须有具体史实支撑；结论须回应题目。

Question 2 [15 marks]

EN: "The Cold War brought more harm than good to Southeast Asian nations." How far do you agree?

中文： "冷战给东南亚国家带来的弊大于利。"你在多大程度上同意？

Answer / 答案（参考答案框架）：

Introduction: I agree only partly. The Cold War brought both serious harm and some benefits to Southeast Asia; on balance, the harm appears greater, but the picture is not one-sided.

For the statement (harm):

  • Proxy wars and conflict: Regions such as Vietnam and Cambodia suffered devastating wars fought largely as proxies of the superpowers, causing immense loss of life and lasting instability.
  • Political interference: Superpowers supported or undermined governments to gain allies, at times preventing genuine self-determination.
  • Economic distortion: Aid and investment often served strategic rather than developmental aims.
  • Division: The region was split by ideological alignments, complicating regional cooperation.

Against the statement (benefits):

  • Economic aid and development: Countries that aligned received substantial aid, infrastructure investment and access to markets, accelerating growth (e.g. during the Vietnam War period, some economies expanded rapidly).
  • Regional cooperation: The shared fear of communism encouraged the formation of ASEAN in 1967, promoting stability and cooperation.
  • Anti-colonial leverage: As Source C of Case Study B suggested, local leaders could leverage superpower rivalry to obtain resources and support for their own national agendas.
  • Security guarantees: Some smaller states obtained defence commitments that deterred external aggression.

Conclusion: Overall, the harm — particularly the proxy wars and loss of life — was severe and long-lasting, so I agree with the statement to a large extent. However, it would be inaccurate to deny that the Cold War also brought some developmental and diplomatic benefits. The judgement depends on the country concerned: for those devastated by conflict, the harm clearly outweighed the good.

中文解析：

步骤1："How far do you agree"题 须双向论证 + 明确判断。

步骤2：支持（弊）：

① 代理人战争（越南、柬埔寨）→ 巨大伤亡与长期动荡；

② 政治干预 → 阻碍真正的自决；

③ 经济扭曲 → 援助服务于战略而非发展；

④ 区域分裂 → 意识形态阵营化。

步骤3：反对（利）：

① 经济援助与基建 → 部分国家加速增长；

② 区域合作 → 1967 年东盟成立，促进稳定；

③ 反殖民筹码 → 本地领导人借对抗获取资源（呼应史料 C）；

④ 安全保障 → 小国获得防务承诺。

步骤4：结论：很大程度上同意——代理人战争的伤害严重且持久；但不宜否认部分发展红利。

步骤5：进阶要点：可指出结论因国而异（受战火蹂躏者弊远大于利），体现评价深度。

步骤6：评分要点：须有明确立场（how far），双向论证需史实具体，避免"两边都有理"的模糊结论。

Question 3 [15 marks]

EN: Explain how Singapore's path to independence differed from that of other Southeast Asian nations. [15]

中文： 解释新加坡的独立道路与其他东南亚国家有何不同。

Answer / 答案（参考答案框架）：

Introduction: Singapore's route to independence differed markedly from that of its neighbours in its timing, method and circumstances.

Point 1 — Singapore achieved independence through negotiation and political union, then separation. Unlike Indonesia, which fought a bitter armed struggle against the Dutch, or Vietnam, which endured prolonged war, Singapore's independence came largely through constitutional negotiation. It joined the Federation of Malaysia in 1963 and became fully independent only upon its expulsion in 1965 — a unique path in which independence was, in a sense, unexpected and involuntary.

Point 2 — The nature of the transfer of power. Whereas several Southeast Asian states inherited substantial territories, resources and administrative structures, Singapore was expelled as a small island city-state with no natural resources, no hinterland and a multiracial population facing serious economic and social challenges.

Point 3 — Ideological position. Many newly independent states aligned with either the communist bloc or the Western bloc, or pursued non-alignment. Singapore, while non-communist, charted a pragmatic, trade-oriented path and later became a founding member of ASEAN, prioritising economic survival and regional cooperation over ideological alignment.

Point 4 — Survival as the central challenge. For most new states, independence meant building a nation from a colonial territory. For Singapore, independence meant survival itself — a theme its leaders emphasised repeatedly in the early years, given its lack of resources and the threat of external pressure.

Conclusion: Singapore's independence was distinctive in that it was achieved through negotiation, arrived unexpectedly through expulsion, and left the new nation with unusually severe structural constraints. Its subsequent emphasis on pragmatism, economic development and regional cooperation reflects these unique origins.

中文解析：

步骤1：比较型论述：明确"新加坡 vs 其他东南亚国家"的差异维度。

步骤2：论点一：方式不同——新加坡靠宪制谈判（1963 年加入马来西亚，1965 年被逐出而独立），区别于印尼的反荷武装斗争、越南的长期战争。

步骤3：论点二：移交条件不同——新加坡是小岛城邦，无资源、无腹地，与继承大片领土资源者迥异。

步骤4：论点三：意识形态立场不同——多数新独立国家站队或走不结盟；新加坡务实、重贸易，后为东盟创始成员。

步骤5：论点四：核心挑战不同——对多数国家是"建国"，对新加坡是"生存"。

步骤6：结论：新加坡的独特性在于——谈判取得 + 意外被逐 + 结构性约束极重，这塑造了其后务实主义与区域合作的路线。

步骤7：评分要点：须紧扣"不同（differed）"，逐点对照，避免只写新加坡单方面情况。
